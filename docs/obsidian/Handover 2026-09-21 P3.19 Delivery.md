## Implemented Contract

- Additive migration `20260921010000_formal_payment_lifecycle` adds typed append-only `OrderLifecycleEvent` history and a nullable email-outbox processing token. Existing order/reservation/refund/email fields remain current-state authority; historical orders receive a visible incomplete-history baseline on their first later transition, not invented backfill.
- Checkout, webhook reconciliation, expiry/cleanup, compensation, manual refund bookkeeping, and email enqueue/retry/acceptance append transactionally consistent events. The organiser order page exposes a bounded, tenant-scoped, paginated timeline with staff attribution.
- Ambiguous Stripe identity cannot mutate an order; paid sessions arriving after ordinary local failure enter compensation review; manual refund blocks automatic compensation recovery. The email worker uses rotating claim-token fencing so a stale worker cannot finalize a reclaimed job.
- No raw webhook payloads, credentials, buyer email addresses, card data, or invite tokens belong in lifecycle facts or audit-style evidence. Stripe remains payment truth; a manual refund flag is not a Stripe refund.
