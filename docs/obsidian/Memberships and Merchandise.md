# Memberships and Merchandise

The commerce families a society sells besides tickets. Orders, lines, history, checkout and webhook routing are shared (T20, T21; see [[Payment Lifecycle]]). This note owns what each family adds. Delivery order: plans (T22), grants, renewal and the roster (T23), offers and My memberships (T24a), member prices and members-only events (T24b), then merchandise (T25 to T28).

## Membership plans (T22)

A plan (`MembershipProduct`) is one fixed term a society sells: name, description, an integer AUD price (0 for free, otherwise $0.50 or more, Stripe's smallest card payment; the same rule applies to merchandise options), a half-open term `[startsAt, endsAt)` and the rights it unlocks, from a whitelist (`member_prices`, `members_only_events`) with an `entitlementVersion`. It is a one-off purchase, never a subscription, and never renews.

- **Dates.** Staff enter a first and last day. The term runs from midnight starting the first day to midnight after the last day, in the society's time zone (`termFromDays`; `daysFromTerm` reverses it), so daylight saving is handled by the zone, not the browser. Presets fill both days: Semester 1 is 1 January to 30 June, Semester 2 is 1 July to 31 December, Full year is the calendar year; every date stays editable. A day with no midnight in the zone is a field error.
- **States.** Draft, published, archived (database), shown as Draft, Published, Ended (published but past its last day) and Archived. Only published plans that have not ended are on sale (`publicPlans`, for T24a). Archive is one way: the plan stops selling and cannot be republished; holders keep their terms. Plans are never deleted.
- **Publish check** (`planReadiness`, the one rule for the button and the API): the plan ends in the future; it does not overlap another published plan of the society (one plan per period, so Semester 1 and a Full year cannot both be on sale for the same months; back-to-back terms are fine because terms are half-open); a paid plan needs payments set up, a free one does not. Saving a published plan re-runs the check for anything but a description edit.
- **Sold plans.** Once any membership order line names a plan (`CommerceOrderLine.membershipProductId`, pending or paid), its name, term, price and rights are locked; only the description changes. A database trigger backs this up and never lets a plan move society. The next year is a new plan.
- **Concurrency.** Every change holds User, then Organisation, then the plan row (`lockOrganisationActor`, then `FOR UPDATE`), with a version check (409 on a stale version). Publishing and editing published plans therefore run one at a time per society, and T23 checkout, which locks the Organisation before reading a plan, never quotes a plan mid-edit.
- **Access.** Staff with live `memberships:manage` (owners and admins) through `/api/orgs/[orgSlug]/memberships` and `/dashboard/memberships`; another society's plan is a 404. Every change is audited (`membership_plan.created`, `updated`, `published`, `archived`).

## Grants, checkout and the roster (T23a)

A `MembershipGrant` is one member's membership from one purchased line: unique per line and in the line's society, with the plan's term and rights copied onto it. Its facts never change; it can be revoked once, with a reason (T23b). Its state comes only from the clock and `revokedAt` (`grantState`): Upcoming before `startsAt`, Active in `[startsAt, endsAt)`, Expired from `endsAt`, Revoked once revoked. No job ever changes it.

- **Checkout.** The membership family is registered with commerce checkout (`lib/memberships/grants.ts`, `membershipFamily`). Body `selection: {planId}`; members only, never guests; one line of quantity 1 at the plan's price. Under the buyer, then society locks, the quote refuses a plan that is not published or has ended or belongs to another society (`plan_unavailable`), dates the member already holds (`term_overlap`), and dates with another checkout still waiting on Stripe (`purchase_in_progress`); each is a 409 with that `reason` and nothing is charged. Renewal is simply buying the next plan: terms are half-open, so a plan starting when the current one ends does not overlap.
- **Fulfilment.** A free total grants inside checkout; a paid one only from the verified webhook. Under the member, society and order locks the grant is created once (a replay finds the line's grant), with a receipt notice in the same transaction. If the member meanwhile got overlapping dates another way, the paid order goes to compensation review (`term_overlap`) and no second grant is made. Lock order: User, Organisation, then the commerce order; plan edits take the same Organisation lock.
- **Roster** (`lib/memberships/roster.ts`, `members:read`, owners and admins): `/dashboard/memberships/members` shows Active, Upcoming, Expired and Revoked grants, newest first with stable pages and name or email search, and Free joins as a separate list (a join gives no member prices; joiners who also hold a membership are marked). `/dashboard/memberships/members/[grantId]` shows the term, rights, source order (Paid or Free and its short reference), the free join, other terms, whether the person is staff and a What happened history (granted, started, ended early); another society's grant is a 404.

## Ending a membership early (T23b)

`lib/memberships/revocation.ts`, under member, society and order locks. Both actions need a reason (8 to 500 characters) and, unless staff MFA is off, a fresh authenticator code; both are audited, email the member (`membership-ended/{grantId}`) and answer a repeat with the first result. Neither moves money.

- **Revoke** (`memberships:manage`, owners and admins): stops a running or upcoming membership now, with no refund (`membership.revoked`). An ended membership can't be revoked. Tickets already bought at a member price keep their price (T24b).
- **Cancel a free membership** (`orders:refund`): the T18c shape. The free order gets `cancelledAt`, `cancelledByUserId` and `cancellationReason` (allowed only on free, paid-status orders), keeps its status and fulfilment, gains a `free_cancelled` history entry, and its grant is revoked with the same reason. Never a refund row. A paid membership is refused (`not_cancellable`): refund it instead.
- **Leaving the society** removes only the free join; the membership stays (regression test).

The member page's Manage this membership card holds the two dialogs; Cancel free membership replaces Revoke for a free membership. Commerce order pages arrive with T29, so the cancellation lives on the member page for now.

## Refunding a paid membership (T23c)

Refund membership on the member page (`orders:refund`; reason, the order's short reference typed back and the authenticator code) starts a whole-order Stripe refund through the shared refund path (see [[Payment Lifecycle]], Whole-Order Refunds). The membership stays active while Stripe processes it (the page shows Refund processing) and is revoked only when Stripe confirms; the member is emailed once. A failed or refused refund leaves it active. Tickets bought at a member price keep their price. Disputes and refunds made in Stripe's dashboard are not yet projected for commerce orders (PAY-003).

**Disputes and refunds made in Stripe (PAY-003).** The member page and the merchandise order page show what Stripe reported that Thunderstrux didn't do, in the ticket order page's words. An open dispute pauses refunds (and hand-over: the order waits on the Pickups Review tab); a lost one ends the membership or leaves nothing to collect; a won one says the society kept the payment. A full refund made in the Stripe dashboard ends the membership or cancels the pickup; a partial one is flagged with Mark reviewed, and Refund then takes only the rest. Orders' Payment review filter includes disputed and unreviewed partly refunded orders. A paid order that couldn't be filled is refunded in full automatically, and the buyer is told so at once. See [[Payment Lifecycle]].

## Offers, buying and My memberships (T24a)

`lib/memberships/offers.ts` decides what members see; checkout re-checks everything under locks.

- **Society page.** `components/organisations/society-header.tsx` is shared by Overview and `/societies/[slug]/memberships`: cover, logo, name, the viewer's `Joined` badge and, separately, `Member until {last day}` while a membership runs, then the section tabs (Memberships only while a plan is on sale). A free join never shows Member until.
- **Offers** (`membershipOffers`): published plans still to end, soonest first, each with the viewer's standing: `held` (their plan; "You're a member until …" or "starts …"), `covered` (another of their memberships overlaps; no button), `pending` (their checkout for those dates is confirming) or `available` (Buy membership, Get free membership, or Renew for {plan} when it starts after a membership they hold ends). Signed out: Sign in to buy membership; unverified: a verify prompt; organisation accounts: no button.
- **Review** (`/societies/[slug]/memberships/[planId]/checkout`, CheckoutShell): verified member accounts only. Term, unlocks, money summary, "Your cover" when it follows a membership they hold, the refund policy and support address. Dates already held or confirming show the refusal instead of the button. The button keeps one idempotency key per intent, follows Stripe, a free fulfilment or a still-confirming attempt, and names each refusal (`term_overlap`, `purchase_in_progress`, `plan_unavailable`, a changed price) with "Nothing has been charged".
- **Return page** (`/memberships/orders/[orderId]`): Stripe's success and cancel URLs for a membership both land here (`?left=1` on cancel). Only the buyer sees it; it shows the order's real state and never fulfils anything. The states are:
  - You haven't paid yet (round E1), while Stripe's page is open and the hold lasts, with Return to payment and Cancel order;
  - confirming with a poller, once Stripe has the payment;
  - You're a member, or upcoming from a date;
  - no payment taken ("You cancelled this order" after Cancel);
  - refunding, when it went to compensation (PAY-003).
- **My memberships** (`/memberships`, personal menu Memberships, `myMemberships`): Active, Upcoming, Expired and Revoked tabs with counts; each card shows the society, plan, dates in the society's zone, unlocks, Paid or Free, what ended it early (a refund, the society's cancellation or revocation with its reason) and Renew for the next published plan that starts after it ends and overlaps none of their memberships there. "Memberships don't renew automatically" stays visible.
- **My societies**: each joined society shows `Joined` and, while a membership runs, `Member until`; leaving says the membership is not affected.

## Member prices (T24b1)

- **Setting it.** Each ticket type has an optional Member price in the builder (`TicketType.memberPrice`, at most the price). The builder warns while no published plan still to end unlocks member prices (`memberPricesOnSale`).
- **Who pays it.** A member account whose running membership in the event's society (unrevoked, started, not ended) carries `member_prices` (`activeEntitlementGrant`). A free join, an upcoming, expired or revoked membership, one without that right, another society's membership and every guest pay the standard price.
- **Checkout.** Under the buyer, event and ticket-type locks the quote uses the member price, so a buyer sending the other price gets the 409 quote change with the price that applies. The order records `membershipGrantId` and `membershipEntitlementVersion` (both or neither; fixed by a trigger) and charges the member price; a member price of zero is a free registration with the grant recorded, even when the society can't take payments.
- **Fulfilment.** The grant is read `FOR SHARE`: a membership revoked or refunded before the payment landed sends the order to compensation review (`member_price_membership_revoked`) and issues no tickets; a membership that simply ended during the 30-minute hold keeps the price. Tickets already issued at a member price are never repriced or voided by a later revocation.
- **Showing it.** `priceForViewer` prices the event page and ticket checkout per viewer: a member sees the member price with the standard one struck through and "Member price through your {plan} membership"; anyone else sees "Members pay $X", that joining doesn't include it, and a link to the society's memberships.

## Members-only events (T24b2)

- **Setting it.** The builder's Access card offers Members only beside Public and Link only. Drafts save; publishing needs a published plan still to end that unlocks members-only events (`eventReadiness` with `membersOnlyReady`, from `entitlementOnSale`), shown as an extra checklist row with a Go to Memberships link and as a warning in the builder. An event published while a plan was on sale stays published after the plan ends; its members keep access while their memberships run.
- **Who can open it.** A verified member account whose running membership of the society (unrevoked, started, not ended) carries `members_only_events`, checked live on every request (`getEventForViewer` in `lib/events/public-events.ts`). A free join, an upcoming, expired or revoked membership, one without that right, another society's membership, guests, staff and society accounts don't count; organisers use the dashboard and Preview.
- **The gate.** Anyone else who opens `/events/[eventId]` gets "Members-only event" naming only the society (name, slug, logo) and why: sign in, verify your email, no active membership ("Joining for free doesn't count"), a membership without members-only events, or one that starts later (with its first day). Tab title "Members-only event · {society}", `noindex`, and the page is rendered per request with `private, no-store`. Drafts, deleted events and wrong ids are still "Page not found".
- **Safety contract.** The gate decision reads only the event's id, status, visibility and society; the event's other fields are read only after access is granted, so nothing about the event can reach the gate's HTML, page data or metadata. Tests put sentinel values in the title, description, place and ticket name and assert their absence for every non-entitled viewer, and on the full signed-out response.
- **Everything else stays hidden.** Explore, home, the society page and its Events list, and `GET /api/public/events/[eventId]` leave members-only events out. Ticket checkout re-checks access under the buyer lock (the lock revocation and refunds also take) and refuses anyone else, guests included, with the same `event_not_found` as a missing event; the checkout page sends them back to the gate. Fulfilment does not re-check: a membership revoked during a valid 30-minute hold still gets the tickets it started buying.
- **The image.** `GET /api/public/assets/[assetId]` serves a members-only event's image only to a member it is open to, with `private, no-store`; it is never on the public, cached path.
- **The member's view.** The full page with "Members only. You can see this because your {society} membership includes members-only events." The confirmation page reads the event for the buyer's own order, so a ticket holder keeps it.

## Merchandise catalogue (T25)

`lib/merchandise/catalogue.ts`, from design round B batch 2. A product (`MerchandiseProduct`) is something a society sells for pickup: a name, an optional description, up to four photos and the options buyers pick. It is draft, published or archived, and never deleted. Nothing sells until T27; the shop arrives with T26.

- **Options** (`MerchandiseVariant`): colour and size (either may be blank; a product with one option leaves both blank), a SKU, an integer AUD price and the stock left. Each saved option carries a version that a sale (T27) or another editor bumps, so a save made from a stale page is a `409` and changes nothing. The same colour and size twice is refused. Up to 50 options.
- **SKUs** are unique in the society for good, including options that stopped selling, so a receipt's SKU always names one option. A blank SKU is made from the name, colour and size (`ENGSOC-HOODIE-NAVY-L`, then `-2` if taken). Decision: unlike the readiness plan's "tenant-unique SKU" field, staff never have to type one; Shopify treats SKUs as optional and Eventbrite and Humanitix add-ons have none, but stock and pickups still need a stable identifier.
- **Photos**: ready `product` images of the same society (`MerchandiseProductImage`, position 0 to 3; the first is the cover), uploaded through the shared asset route. Alt text is derived ("{product}, photo 1 of 3") rather than asked for. Photos count as references for asset cleanup and deletion, and are public once the product is published.
- **Publish check** (`productReadiness`, the one rule for the button and the API): a photo; an option buyers can get, meaning in stock and either free or payable (like event ticket types, free options in stock let a product publish before payments are set up); and the society's pickup details. Decision: a photo is required although Eventbrite and Humanitix add-ons don't need one, because clothing is bought on its look. A published product must keep a photo and an option; stock may run out and prices may change (receipts keep the price paid).
- **Removing an option**: deleted while the product has never been on sale (`firstPublishedAt` is null); after that it stops selling (`archivedAt`) and keeps its SKU.
- **Archive and restore**: archiving stops sales and hides the product; Restore brings it back as a draft. Decision (changed after round B2 approval): reversible, unlike membership plans, because a product is often sold again next semester and Shopify lets you unarchive for the same reason.
- **Pickup details** are one society-wide text (Society profile, `Organisation.pickupPolicy`), shown with every product, the basket and receipts; a society can't clear them while it has products on sale.
- **Concurrency**: every change holds User, then Organisation, then the product row, then its options in ascending id (`lockOrganisationActor`, then `FOR UPDATE`), then any photo assets. T27 checkout locks options in the same ascending order and never the product, so the two can't deadlock.
- **Access**: live `merchandise:manage` (owners and admins) through `/api/orgs/[orgSlug]/merchandise` and `/dashboard/merchandise` (Community menu). A member who isn't staff, and another society's slug, get `404`; staff of this society without the permission get `403`. Changes are audited (`merchandise_product.created`, `updated`, `published`, `archived`, `restored`).

## Shop and basket (T26)

`lib/merchandise/shop.ts` (server) and `lib/merchandise/basket.ts` (browser), from design round B batch 2.

- **What buyers see**: published products of the society only, with their photos and the options still on sale; drafts, archived products and options that stopped selling never appear. The society page shows a Merchandise tab while it has products on sale (`hasShop`). Cards show the cover photo, the option summary, "From $X" when prices differ, "Only N left" at 5 or fewer, and Sold out. Stock shown is what's left after checkout holds (T27a); it is advice, and checkout decides under locks.
- **Product page** (`/societies/[slug]/merchandise/[productId]`): photo gallery with thumbnails, labelled colour and size radio groups (sold-out options stay visible, disabled, with "sold out"; a price shows beside a size when prices differ), a quantity stepper capped at 10 and at what's left, Add to basket, and the pickup, refund and contact details before any checkout.
- **Basket** (`/societies/[slug]/merchandise/basket`): one society's option ids and quantities in this browser's storage, never prices or names. Repeats merge; at most 10 different options and 10 of each. Adding another society's item asks to keep that basket (with a link to it) or clear it. Every view re-quotes through `POST /api/merchandise/quote`: options no longer sold leave the basket with a notice, quantities above what's left are lowered with a note, sold-out lines must be removed, and the quote's version changes with any price, quantity or line so checkout can refuse a stale basket.
- **Checkout merchandise** opens the checkout page (T27b), unless a line has sold out; the basket notes that prices and stock are checked again and that items are held for 30 minutes while paying.
- **Decisions** (against Shopify, Eventbrite and Humanitix): a standalone shop and basket rather than add-ons inside ticket checkout (as Eventbrite and Humanitix do), because societies sell merchandise outside events; the basket lives on the device, as Shopify carts do, with no account needed to browse; low stock is shown only at 5 or fewer (Humanitix hides counts, Eventbrite can show remaining), to help without inventing urgency; sold-out options stay visible rather than vanishing, so buyers know the size exists.

## Selling merchandise (T27a)

`lib/merchandise/sale.ts`, the merchandise commerce family (`merchandiseFamily`), registered with commerce checkout (see [[Payment Lifecycle]]). The checkout and order pages are T27b, below.

- **Who can buy.** A verified member account or a verified guest's checkout session (guest email verification, T16a). Body `selection: {items: [{variantId, quantity}], quoteVersion}`: 1 to 10 different options of one society, 1 to 10 of each, each option once, and the version of the quote the buyer reviewed.
- **Checkout.** Under the buyer lock, then the basket's options in ascending id, the quote refuses an option no longer sold or of another society (`unavailable`), more than is left (`sold_out`, or `not_enough` with the number left), and a basket whose prices or quantities changed (`quote_changed`, from the version); the total must also match. Each refusal is a 409 and charges nothing. Nothing is lowered at checkout: the basket re-quotes and shows the change instead. Each line records its option (`CommerceOrderLine.merchandiseVariantId`), name, option label and price.
- **Holds.** A paid basket holds its stock for 30 minutes, the Stripe Session's own expiry. A hold is not a row of its own: the lines of a pending Stripe order before its `expiresAt` hold their units (`heldByVariant`), so paying, failing or expiring the order ends the hold and the two can never disagree. The shop, the quote, checkout and fulfilment all subtract holds; a lost Stripe answer keeps the order, so its hold stays until it expires.
- **Fulfilment.** A free total completes inside checkout; a paid one only from the verified webhook. Under the option and order locks, every line is taken from stock once (`remaining` down, the option's version up, so a staff page open since the sale must reload) or none is, with one receipt (`merchandise-receipt/{orderId}`) listing the items, the total, the short order reference and the society's pickup details. A guest's receipt also carries a link back to the order (see Guests and their order, below). An option that stopped selling during a valid hold is still honoured. A payment whose hold lapsed before it landed, when the stock has since gone, goes to compensation review (`sold_out`) and takes nothing; a payment on an order already expired goes to review as for every family.
- **Stock edits.** Saving an option with less stock than checkouts are holding is refused on the field ("3 are held in checkouts right now, so stock can't go below 3."). Catalogue edits lock the product, then its options; checkout locks only options, in the same ascending order, so the two can't deadlock.
- **Decisions** (against Eventbrite, Humanitix and Shopify): holds last 30 minutes, matching tickets and Stripe's shortest Session expiry, where Eventbrite and Humanitix hold for a checkout timer and Shopify holds nothing until payment (oversells are worse for one-off society stock than a short hold); guests may buy merchandise, as on Eventbrite and Humanitix; a free basket completes at once with no Stripe step; a basket mixing free and paid options goes through Stripe with the free ones on the order but not on Stripe's line items; holds are derived from pending orders rather than kept in a reservation table, so they can't drift from the order they belong to (the readiness plan named a `MerchandiseReservation` table; the migration is `merchandise_sale` instead); T27 was split by layer, not into free and paid, because holds exist only for paid orders and the paid path is T21's shared machinery.

## Checkout and order pages (T27b)

From design round B batch 2 (MerchCheckoutB2, GuestMerchB2Mobile, MerchResultB2Mobile, ShopStatesB2).

- **Checkout page** (`/societies/[slug]/merchandise/checkout`, CheckoutShell, `noindex`): the basket lives on the device, so the page re-quotes it. A verified member account reviews it signed in; an unverified one is asked to verify first; a society account goes back to the basket. Anyone signed out lands on the guest email step ("Where should we send your receipt?", with Sign in instead), and the emailed link brings them back to the review as a verified guest. The review shows the items, the pickup details, where the receipt goes (a guest's address marked Confirmed), the 30-minute hold, the refund policy and the support address, then Pay $X or Confirm free order. A basket that changed since it was last shown (a price, fewer left, sold out, no longer sold) is not paid from here: the page says so and links back to the basket, which fixes it. The button keeps one idempotency key per intent, follows Stripe, a free order or a still-confirming attempt, and names each refusal ("Some items changed", "An item is no longer sold", "Checkout didn't start") with "Nothing has been charged".
- **Order page** (`/merchandise/orders/[orderId]`, `noindex`): Stripe's success and cancel URLs and free orders land here. Only the member who bought it, the guest checkout session that bought it, or a purchase link for that order can open it; anyone else gets not found, and someone signed out without a guest session is sent to sign in. It shows the order's real state and never fulfils anything:
  - You haven't paid yet (Waiting for payment, round E1) while Stripe's page is open and the hold lasts, with Return to payment and Cancel order;
  - Confirming your payment (with a poller) once Stripe has it;
  - No payment taken (cancelled, or the hold ended; the basket is kept);
  - We're refunding your payment (compensation, PAY-003);
  - or Order confirmed with Paid or Free, the lines, the short order reference and the pickup details. The basket for that society is cleared once the order is confirmed.
- **Guests and their order.** `GET /api/commerce/orders/[orderId]` and the order page answer a signed-out guest only through `canOpenGuestCommerceOrder`: a checkout session opens the merchandise orders bought in that session, never another session's of the same email, and a purchase session opens the one order its link names (`GuestAccessGrant.commerceOrderId`). A guest's receipt is a `guest_order_link` email (not a society `business_notice`) whose link opens that order on any device, once, for 24 hours; Find your purchase (`/guest/recover`) sends a fresh link per paid guest merchandise order as well as per ticket order. A purchase session can't buy. The order page tells a guest that Find your purchase sends a fresh link; the guest review says the receipt carries the link.
- **Guest links, decisions** (against Eventbrite, Humanitix and Shopify, which all email guests an order link): one email, not a receipt and a separate link; the link is single-use and lasts 24 hours like a ticket link, with Find your purchase as the way back after that, rather than a long-lived bearer link in the inbox. Because the receipt is now a guest link email, a guest's failed receipt is not in the society's failed-notification list for requeue; Find your purchase replaces it. Find your purchase also supersedes any unused link for an order, including an unsent receipt's (its queued email is cancelled and the new email carries the link).
- **Not yet shown:** merchandise in Purchases arrives with T29, so the pages don't promise it. Pickup states arrived with T28a (below). Guest merchandise links (above) were brought forward from T29.
- **Decisions** (against Eventbrite, Humanitix and Shopify): signed-out buyers go straight to the email step with a sign-in link, as Shopify's checkout asks for an email first, rather than a choice page; the review never lowers quantities itself but sends the buyer back to the basket, where the change is explained once.

## Pickups (T28a)

`lib/merchandise/pickups.ts`, from design round B batch 2 (PickupsB2, PickupsB2Mobile, PickupActionsB2). T28 is split: T28a is the pickup workflow; T28b adds returns that restock once, merchandise refunds and cancelling free orders.

- **State.** A merchandise order gets `CommerceOrder.pickupStatus` `pending` when it is fulfilled (paid by the webhook or free in checkout); staff move it to `ready`, then `collected`. It is separate from payment: the buyer's page shows a Payment badge and a Pickup badge (Preparing, Ready to collect, Collected) with a three-step tracker. Orders under payment review never get a pickup state.
- **Pickups tab** (`/dashboard/merchandise/pickups`, `merchandise:fulfil`, owners and admins; beside Products, which shows "Pickups · N to prepare"): To prepare, Ready to collect, Collected and Needs review, with counts, search by order reference, name or email, and pages of 25. To prepare and Ready list oldest first (the order they came in; decided at round B2 pass 2), Collected and Needs review newest first. Rows show the reference, buyer (and Guest), items, and when it was ordered, marked ready or handed over and by whom; a table from 768px, cards with full-width buttons below.
- **Mark ready and Mark collected** (`POST /api/orgs/[slug]/merchandise/pickups/[orderId]`, body `{step, expectedVersion}`): each is confirmed in a dialog listing the items (Mark collected names the buyer to check). Under the staff, society and order locks the step needs a paid order not under review, refunded or cancelled, the previous state (collected needs ready first) and the order's current `version`. It moves the state, bumps the version, and commits a `pickup_ready` or `pickup_collected` history entry with the staff member, an audit row (`merchandise_order.ready`, `.collected`) and one buyer email (`merchandise-ready/{orderId}` with the pickup details, `merchandise-collected/{orderId}`) together. A page opened before someone else's step gets a 409 naming who did it and when, and nothing changes or is emailed twice. The page then shows the result from the server.
- **Decisions** (against Eventbrite, Humanitix and Shopify): pickup is tracked per order, not per item, like Shopify's local pickup (Ready for pickup, then Picked up), because societies hand over a bag at a table; collected needs ready first, so the buyer is always told before the table; there is no undo, since an email has gone (a mistaken step is corrected by staff in person, and returns arrive in T28b); the staff order page arrived with T28b (below).

## Returns, refunds and free cancellation (T28b)

`lib/merchandise/after-sale.ts`, from design round B batch 2 (MerchRefundB2, MerchOrderB2).

- **Staff order page** (`/dashboard/merchandise/orders/[orderId]`, `merchandise:fulfil`; opened from the order reference on the Pickups tab): Items (option, SKU, price each, how many returned), Pickup (the step and Mark ready or Mark collected), What happened (the order's history with who and when), Buyer, Money (paid or free, refunded, the fee for `orders:read`) and Returns. Another society's order is not found. Unified Orders (T29) will link here too.
- **Refund** (`orders:refund`; reason, the reference typed back and the authenticator code): the whole payment through the shared commerce refund path (T23c, `requestCommerceRefund`), with the same fence and Stripe checks. While Stripe processes it the order is on hold (off the Pickups tab, "Refund processing"); when Stripe confirms, a pickup not yet collected is cancelled (it stays off the waiting stages) and the buyer is emailed once ("The pickup is cancelled"). A collected order that is refunded stays in Collected history. A refund never records a return and never restocks.
- **Cancel a free order** (`orders:refund`; reason and code), the T18c shape: `cancelledAt`, who and why on the order, a `free_cancelled` history entry, an audit row and one email, never a refund row. A collected free order can't be cancelled (record a return); a paid one is refunded instead; stock isn't put back.
- **Record a return** (`merchandise:fulfil`; `POST .../merchandise/orders/[orderId]/returns`): a `MerchandiseReturn` row for one line, at most what was bought less earlier returns, with a reason and a choice: put it back in stock or not (it can't be sold again). Restocking happens once, as the row is written, under staff, society, option and order locks; the client's operation key makes a repeated request a replay. History `return_recorded` and an audit row; no email and no money.
- **Buyer's page**: Refunded ("Not collected, nothing to pick up" with the amount and bank timing), Refund processing, or Cancelled; a collected order keeps its Collected step.
- **Decisions** (against Eventbrite, Humanitix and Shopify): whole-order refunds only (buyer self-service and partial refunds stay out of scope, as for tickets); a refund and a return are separate records, as in Shopify, because societies often refund without getting the item back and take items back without refunding (an exchange); restocking is a staff choice per return, never automatic, because a returned hoodie may be worn or damaged; a free order can be cancelled only before collection.

## Purchases tabs (T29a)

`/purchases` (personal menu Purchases) has Tickets, Memberships and Merchandise tabs with counts, from design round B batch 2 (PurchasesB2). Tickets is the default and keeps its rows and `/purchases/tickets/[orderId]` links. Memberships lists the member's membership orders (plan, society, term, payment badge, total, date) linking to `/memberships/orders/[id]`; Merchandise lists merchandise orders ("{first item} and N more", society, order reference) with payment and pickup as separate badges ("Not collected" after a refund or cancellation before pickup) linking to `/merchandise/orders/[id]`. Every list is scoped to the member in its query (`getOwnTicketPurchases`, `getOwnCommerceOrders` with a family) and pages newest first by the shared timestamp cursor; the tab is in the URL (`?family=memberships|merchandise`). Commerce orders reuse the ticket purchase projection (`ticketOrderProjection`, `buyerPurchaseBadge`), which reads only facts both kinds of order carry, so Paid, Free, Refunded, Refund processing, Cancelled, Payment issue and Didn't complete mean the same in every tab and on the staff merchandise order page. Organiser Orders tabs are T29b.

## Organiser Orders tabs (T29b)

`/dashboard/orders` (`orders:read`) has Tickets, Memberships and Merchandise tabs, from design round B batch 2 (OrdersB2). The commerce tabs come from `listOrganisationCommerceOrders`: the society's orders only (scoped in the query), checkouts still waiting on Stripe left out, status pills All, Paid, Free, Refunded, Payment review and Expired, buyer email, plan or product (`commerceProductOptions`) and date filters, newest first with stable pages on the shared cursor. Rows show the buyer (Guest marked) and order reference, items or plan and term, total, payment and, for merchandise, pickup ("On hold" under review, "Not collected" after a refund or cancellation before pickup). A merchandise row opens `/dashboard/merchandise/orders/[id]` and a granted membership opens the member's page; both pages now accept `orders:read` or their own permission (`requireManagementPage` takes a list), so a finance manager can refund from them but sees no Mark ready, return or revoke action. A finance manager arriving from Orders gets an Orders breadcrumb.

## Renewal reminders and expiry notices (T30)

The notification worker queues a renewal reminder seven days before a membership ends and an expiry notice once it has ended, each once per grant. The reminder is the platform's only optional email: members turn it off in Account settings › Notifications. Neither goes when the member already has a later membership with the society, and neither extends a term; the reminder names the next plan on sale, if there is one. A revoked membership gets its revocation notice instead of an expiry notice. Details in [[Email Delivery Implementation]].
